How to request a glaze 💕

When you add something to your cart, you'll see a personalization box before checkout! Just type in the glaze name (or names — mix and match is totally fine to ask about) and any other requests you have. I'll reach out if I have questions. Not sure which to pick? Feel free to send me an email and I can help!
